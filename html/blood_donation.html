1 Donation Can Save Up to 3 Lives

Every Drop Counts. Donate Blood Today.

Please review the safety guidelines below before submitting your donation details through the MediNexus Blood Bank.

1. Drink plenty of water (at least 500ml) and avoid caffeine or alcohol 24 hours prior to donation.

2. Eat a healthy, iron-rich meal beforehand and avoid high-fat food items.

3. Get a full 7 to 8 hours of restful sleep the night before your donation.

4. Donors must be 18 to 65 years old and weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs).

5. Maintain at least 56 days (8 weeks) between successive whole blood donations.

6. Must be free of cold/flu, active antibiotics, or tattoos/piercings within the last 6 months.
