Organ Donation — Terms & Conditions Agreement

This document constitutes the official legal consent and terms of agreement for voluntary organ and/or tissue donation under the authority of MediNexus Hospital & LifeCare Network. Please read all terms carefully before completing and signing this form.

Terms & Conditions of Organ Donation

1. Voluntary Consent
Organ and tissue donation is entirely voluntary. No person shall be coerced, pressured, or financially incentivized to donate organs or tissues. The donor or the donor's authorized representative may withdraw consent at any time prior to the commencement of organ retrieval surgery.

2. Eligible Organs & Tissues for Donation
MediNexus Hospital facilitates donation of the following organs and tissues (as medically feasible and clinically determined): Heart, Kidneys (up to 2), Liver, Lungs (up to 2), Pancreas, Intestine, Corneas, Skin grafts, Bone and bone marrow, Heart valves, and Blood vessels. Not all organs listed may be suitable at the time of donation; final suitability is determined by the transplant team.

3. Brain Death or Cardiac Death Determination
Organ donation following death requires a lawful and independent clinical declaration of brain death (brainstem death) or cardiac death by at least two qualified registered medical officers, neither of whom is a member of the transplant team. Family consent is sought only after such declaration is made.

4. No Commercial Transaction
It is strictly prohibited under the Human Organ and Tissue Transplantation Act to buy, sell, or commercially deal in human organs or tissues. Donation is solely an act of voluntary charity. Any commercial arrangement shall void this consent and attract criminal prosecution.

5. Rights of the Donor's Family
The immediate family (spouse, adult children, parents, siblings) of the deceased has the right to be fully informed of the donation process. Where the deceased has registered as a donor, the family's objection is noted, but lawful registration by the deceased takes precedence in applicable jurisdictions. MediNexus Hospital will always seek to act with sensitivity and transparency.

6. Medical Suitability & Screening
All donated organs and tissues undergo rigorous medical and pathological screening for transmissible diseases (including HIV, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, and other infections) prior to transplantation. MediNexus Hospital reserves the right to decline organs that do not meet clinical safety thresholds.

7. Anonymity & Confidentiality
The identity of the donor shall not be disclosed to the recipient family, and vice versa, without the express written consent of both parties. All records pertaining to organ donation are maintained under strict confidentiality as per applicable data protection and health information laws.

8. No Guarantee of Utilization
Consent to donation does not guarantee that all or any specific organs will be used. Organ allocation is governed by the National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organization (NOTTO) priority and medical matching criteria. Unused organs are treated with dignity and respect.

9. No Impact on Quality of Medical Care
The decision to donate organs shall have no bearing whatsoever on the quality, urgency, or standard of medical care provided to the patient. The treating team is entirely separate from the transplant and procurement team.

10. Living Donation
Living donors may donate a kidney or a portion of the liver to an immediate blood relative or a close emotional relative (as approved by the Authorization Committee). Living donors must be adults (18 years or older), of sound mind, and must provide independent uncoerced consent after counseling.

11. Consent by Family Representative
In cases where the patient is deceased or unable to provide consent, the nearest legally authorized family representative (spouse first, then adult children, then parents, then adult siblings) may sign this consent form on the patient's behalf.

12. Organ Donation Card
Completing this form shall register the donor with the MediNexus Organ Donor Registry. A digital donor card and a registration confirmation will be dispatched to the provided contact details.

13. Legal Governing Framework
This consent is governed by the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act, 1994 (as amended) and applicable State rules, regulations of the National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation (NOTTO), and MediNexus Hospital institutional policy.

14. Right to Withdraw
A registered donor may withdraw their registration at any time during their lifetime by submitting a signed written revocation to the MediNexus Organ Donor Registry. Withdrawal is effective only upon confirmation of receipt and processing.
